## Company separation rules

1. Resolve the company and canonical repository before making changes.
2. Official Intelligence is the parent operating layer; it does not silently absorb product code, customers, data, billing, or legal identity.
3. YOAI, Revolution Intelligence, GummyBearGummy, BitForge, and the standalone assistant remain separate products unless Grant Gazvoda explicitly approves a specific migration.
4. Never share Stripe accounts, customers, price IDs, webhook secrets, databases, authentication sessions, customer records, production secrets, or domains by default.
5. Reusable security, CI, observability, design, and provider-abstraction patterns may be shared only with explicit ownership and boundaries.
6. Archived repositories are provenance and reference assets, not production sources.
7. Do not merge historical YOAI branches wholesale. Reconcile behavior and tests against current `main`.
8. Treat `website-project-homepage` and `bitfordge` as separate BitForge roles until a formal consolidation is approved.
9. Treat `revolution-base` as an independent assistant until renamed or formally reassigned.
10. Do not claim Higgsfield independence for repositories that still depend on Higgsfield packages, deployment injection, authentication hooks, or provider contracts.
11. Store no secrets, customer data, private evidence, credentials, or production values in this file.
12. Justice for Gina / Mom Murdered remains outside business repositories and must never be copied into this map.
